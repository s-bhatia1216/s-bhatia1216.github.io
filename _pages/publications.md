### Current

**Self-Organizing Swarms & Robotics Lab** · PI: Prof. Radhika Nagpal · Aug 2026 – present
Building a fleet of Tumblenauts microgravity inspection robots with a PhD student, ahead of a ZeroG parabolic flight test in November 2026. I designed the tumbling-target motion planner, which generates stable approach and grasp trajectories under free-floating, unactuated tumble dynamics with no reliable gravity reference.

**Beeson Group** · Advisor: Prof. Ryne Beeson · Aug 2026 – present
Physics-informed neural networks for tropical cyclone vortex initialization. The network is trained to fit observations and the governing vortex dynamics jointly, with physical constraints built into the loss, to improve on purely data-driven baselines.
