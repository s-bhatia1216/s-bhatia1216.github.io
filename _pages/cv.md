**Two-time Apple intern in AI performance**, making ML inference run faster on next-generation Apple Silicon. Previously a software development engineer at Johns Hopkins APL, a machine learning engineer at Estée Lauder, and a space software engineer at EMTech Space in Athens, Greece.

I work at the intersection of control theory, machine learning, and hardware, and I am an aspiring leader who is energized by people: I co-led the **95+ member Princeton Rocketry Club** and served as project manager for a **16-person team designing a MIDEX-class heliophysics space mission**. Give me a hard problem and a team, and I will find the constraint that matters and build past it.
