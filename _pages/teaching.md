Some of my favorite hours have been spent on the other side of the desk. Whether it's a student finally seeing why eigenvectors matter or a new club member launching their first rocket, I love helping people get to the "oh, I get it" moment. Teaching and volunteering have been part of my life since long before college, and they are a big part of why I love working with people.

## **Teaching at Princeton**

#### Undergraduate Course Assistant, Linear Systems (EGR 154)

_Keller Center for Engineering Education · Spring 2026_

- Led weekly office hours and problem-solving sessions for **100+ students**, reinforcing the linear algebra and systems theory at the heart of the course: eigendecomposition, the singular value decomposition, state-space models, and frequency-domain analysis.
- Graded problem sets with detailed written feedback, and worked with the graduate TAs to spot the concepts students were struggling with across submissions and address them in sessions.

#### Undergraduate Course Assistant, Linear Systems and Intro to Computer Science

_Department of Computer Science · Spring and Fall 2024_

- Led weekly office hours for **150+ students**, connecting linear algebra (eigendecomposition, SVD, PCA) to real computer science problems like image compression and dimensionality reduction.
- Helped faculty design problem sets and exam questions, contributing to a 5% improvement in the Linear Systems final exam average.

#### Workshop Instructor, Princeton Rocketry Club

_Fall 2022 to Fall 2024_

- Taught new members each semester the skills to build their own rockets: CAD in SolidWorks, flight simulation in OpenRocket, composite fabrication, and rocket assembly.
