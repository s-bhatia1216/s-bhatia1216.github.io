**May 2026** &nbsp;|&nbsp; _Three.js r128, HTML/CSS/JS, Orbital Mechanics_

- Built a browser-based LEO constellation simulator as the MAE 426 (Rocket Propulsion) capstone, exploring the orbital architecture for a hypothetical 1 GW solar-powered space-based data center.
- Real-time 3D orbital simulation in Three.js with no build step and zero dependencies; adjustable orbital planes, inclination, altitude, and phase offset, with Starlink, OneWeb, and Polar presets.
- Coverage percentage, gap time, orbital period, and ground footprint are computed entirely client-side for interactive design-space exploration.
