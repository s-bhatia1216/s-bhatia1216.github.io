**Apr – May 2024** &nbsp;|&nbsp; _Java, Clustering, Decision Stump, Kruskal MST_

- Combined graph-based clustering (Kruskal's MST) with a boosted decision-stump weak learner to classify anomalous transactions, reaching 98% accuracy on held-out data.
- Tuned the decision threshold for a low false-negative operating point, since missed fraud costs more than false alarms.
