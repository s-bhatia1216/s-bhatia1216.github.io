**Aug – Dec 2024** &nbsp;|&nbsp; _Python, NumPyro, JAX, StatsModels, Pandas_

- Developed a Bayesian Regression Discontinuity Design to estimate the causal effect of surprise gift campaigns on future spending, using 50,000+ anonymized transactions from The Estée Lauder Companies.
- Fit the model with MCMC (NumPyro/JAX) and informative priors, reducing credible interval widths by 15% and producing posterior distributions over gift-driven revenue lift for marketing budget allocation.
